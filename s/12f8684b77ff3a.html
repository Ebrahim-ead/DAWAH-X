Female Slaves and Wives: Sexual Purity in Islam

From Muslims·Tahsin Arafat, LLM·2025-08-26

Although the term “female slave” does not actually exist in Islam, historically we can see that the image we generally portray in our minds when we say male and female slave is actually related to Christians [1] and Hindus [2] . So since the common term is “female slave,” I am using this word. Today I want to make several comparative reviews between wives and female slave in Islam.

What we generally call marriage is called Nikah (نكاح) or Tazweej (تزويج) in Islamic terminology. Its lexical meaning is: union, pairing. The primary and main purpose of Nikah is sexual union. Sexuality is a basic human need. It must be fulfilled, and at the same time, it must also be controlled. The legal framework for fulfilling this need and simultaneously controlling it is Nikah, whose purpose is to legitimize the union of two genders.
