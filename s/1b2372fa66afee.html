Explanation of Bukhari 5825 Ḥadīth

From Muslims·Ashraful Nafiz

What is the explanation of this Ḥadīth, especially the part where ʿĀʾishah رضي الله عنها said, "I have not seen any woman suffering as much as the believing women. Look! Her skin is greener than her clothes!" Ḥadīth: Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 5825

— Naqib ar-Rahman

In this hadith, a woman came to the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) to plead for her husband who beat her, and 'Ā'ishah (may Allah be pleased with her) when saw the marks of the beating on her and said that in astonishment. In Details - https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/227744

And due to the injury, yellow or green color stains may be created in the injured area after few days, you can see the details from here - https://www.thehealthy.com/first-aid/what-the-color-of-your-bruise-is-trying-to-tell-you/

— Ashraful Nafiz
