It is mentioned in Brittanica:

“The name’s origin can be traced to the earliest Semitic writings in which the word for God was il or el, the latter being used in the Hebrew Bible (Old Testament). Allah is the standard Arabic word for God and is used by Arabic-speaking Christians and Jews as well as by Muslims regardless of their native tongue.” [1]
